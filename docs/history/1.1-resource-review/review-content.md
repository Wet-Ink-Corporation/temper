## Claims verified correct (a sample)

- **Panicking-APIs table (1.98.1, overflow checks on).** Confirmed: `to_digit`/`is_digit` panic for radix outside 2..=36, `char::from_digit` only above 36, and `from_str_radix` for both. `BTreeMap::range` panics only on a non-empty map. `next_multiple_of(0)`, `div_ceil(0)`, negative `isqrt`, `ilog2(0)`, `ilog` with base 1, `Ord::clamp` and NaN `f64::clamp` panic. So do `chunks`, `windows` and `step_by` with 0, and `as_chunks`/`array_windows` with 0 (a runtime `assert!` in the source). `Duration::new`, `from_mins` and `sum` overflow panic, while `Instant - Instant` saturates. `HashMap::get_disjoint_mut` panics on duplicate keys, and the slice form returns `Err`. Also confirmed: `rotate_left(k > len)`, `copy_from_slice` on length mismatch, `Vec::insert(len)` is fine, a NUL in a thread name panics, `to_string` with an erroring `Display` panics, and `strict_add` panics.
- **Lints.** All 96+ clippy lint names the standard cites exist in 1.98 (a rename probe works: `thread_local_initializer_can_be_made_const` becomes `missing_const_for_thread_local`). `chunks_exact_to_as_chunks` and `unbuffered_bytes` are on by default. `needless_collect` and `significant_drop_in_scrutinee` are nursery.
- **ASY-14 scrutinee table.** Let chains and if-let guards are silent. `for` and `match` fire.
- **Other lint behaviour.** `wildcard_imports` ignores `pub use m::*`. `or_fun_call` catches `ok_or(V(format!(..)))`. `map_err_ignore` misses `|_e|`. `indexing_slicing` misses `map[&k]` and `deque[i]`. `disallowed-methods` catches `Scope::spawn` and catches `thread::spawn` in tests. `unchecked_time_subtraction` suggests `.checked_sub(..).unwrap()`. `private_bounds` fires on `pub(crate)` seals. `future_not_send` fires without `T: Sync`. `disallowed_types` does not flag `foldhash::HashMap`.
- **Cargo.** `build.warnings` is ignored by 1.96 and honoured by 1.98.1, including under `cargo doc`. A member's `default-features = false` is a hard error. A member `[profile]` only warns, and `build.warnings` does not catch it.
- **Stabilization versions.** The feature table (1.88–1.97) matches the release notes: `from_mins` and `floor_char_boundary` in 1.91, `downgrade` in 1.92, `fmt::from_fn` and `pop_front_if` in 1.93, `array_windows` in 1.94, `update`/`try_update`, `cold_path`, `push_mut`, `cfg_select!` and if-let guards in 1.95, `assert_matches!` in 1.96 (`use std::assert_matches;` works on 1.96).
- **Dependencies.** tokio's `task::coop::consume_budget` path exists. `TaskTracker::wait` has Acquire/Release ordering.
